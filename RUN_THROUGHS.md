### 5. O2 — Maximum Amount: Lender Sanction vs. Borrower-Safe Amount
- **Lender-Likely Sanction Amount:** **₹14,00,000 – ₹15,50,000**
  - *Lender Underwriting Basis:* Commercial banks apply an aggressive **55%–60% gross FOIR** (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) on Tier-1 corporate salaried earners. Because Priya earns ₹1,10,000 with a 780 CIBIL score, bank algorithms permit total debt service up to ₹66,000/mo. Subtracting her ₹14,000 car EMI, banks calculate a surplus capacity of ₹52,000/mo and will enthusiastically approve sanction letters exceeding ₹15 Lakhs.
- **Borrower-Safe Amount:** **₹4,50,000 – ₹5,00,000**
  - *Borrower Prudence Basis:* The copilot limits safe total debt service to a conservative **35% FOIR** ceiling for discretionary personal loans. Total safe debt service is capped at ₹38,500/mo (`₹1,10,000 × 35%`). Subtracting the ongoing ₹14,000 car loan leaves an incremental safe EMI ceiling of **₹24,500/mo**. Over 36 months, this corresponds to a safe borrowing band of ₹4.5L–₹5.0L.
- **Which Amount the Borrower Should Use:**
  - **The borrower must strictly use the Borrower-Safe Amount (capped at ₹5,00,000).**
  - *Explanation from the App:*
